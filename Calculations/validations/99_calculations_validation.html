Constant 10% Tariff Scenario Results

Constant 10% Scenario: This counterfactual analysis applies a uniform 10% tariff increase across all products and countries, providing a baseline comparison for understanding the relative impacts of actual trade war policies.

These results help isolate the effects of tariff magnitude from sector-specific targeting, showing how a broad-based tariff policy would affect different economic sectors.

Effects vs Import Rankings Comparison

This will help us determine and validate our thoughts on the correctness of the mapping. A few things to keep in mind going forward. First, the effects calculated are inclusive of shares of personal consumption, while import values are not. Second, the BEA includes services imports in their calculations, while the Census Trade data does not, so some countries may have higher effects because of services related trade. Third, as we have discovered, there is a mismatch between the Census' definition of HS -> NAICS -> BEA crosswalks and the BEA's HS -> BEA crosswalk, which leads to countries from regions having lower effects than expected (e.g. Taiwan).
